## Title

Aftercare Loop — Understand, Verify and Follow Through on Your Care (2-minute demo)

## Description

Aftercare Loop helps women understand their after-visit summaries, check their understanding, and follow through on their care.

In this 2-minute demo, a fictional patient, Maya, pastes her PCOS after-visit summary. Aftercare Loop:
• explains it in plain language and defines medical terms
• keeps what her clinician documented separate from general education, and links every source
• traces each documented next step back to the exact sentence in her summary ("View source")
• holds back anything it can't match word for word: for another fictional patient, Priya, one paraphrased step lands under "Needs clarification", marked "Not verified"
• lets her ask Kat, her health companion, to help prepare questions for her care team
• runs a gentle teach-back check. If she misunderstands, Kat explains it another way and she tries again
• builds a source-traceable action plan with barrier-aware suggestions, such as asking the clinic about telehealth when transportation is hard
• gives her an editable summary to bring to her next appointment

Deterministic safety rules run before any AI response. The evaluation page reports results from automated, reproducible tests on synthetic patients. These are not clinical validation, there were no real users, and they do not establish real-world health outcomes.

The demo runs in Scripted Demo Mode, so the responses shown are predefined for demonstration and labeled that way in the app.

Statistics on the problem card come from the team's evidence brief: Kessels, J R Soc Med 2003; Laws et al., PLoS ONE 2018; Kutner et al., NCES 2006 (NAAL 2003); Rodin et al., J Womens Health 2019. Full citations are shown on screen.

Built by Team HW1: Stephanie Kim, Ava Amidei, Kemigisha Sherifah Muzungu M, and Payton Mendygral.
Mentors: Rhea John and Pavel Stepanov.

Narration voice generated with text-to-speech (Gemini TTS).

⚠️ Fictional data. Not medical advice. All patients, documents and results shown are synthetic. Aftercare Loop does not diagnose, change medications or replace your care team. In an emergency, call your local emergency number (911 in the US). For suicide & crisis support in the US, call 988.
