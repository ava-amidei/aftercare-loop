# Aftercare Loop — Evidence Brief

**Purpose:** citable facts for the pitch deck and demo video. Every claim below lists the exact figure, the source (authors, year, venue or agency), and the URL/DOI actually retrieved during research on 2026-11-07. Items that could not be confirmed against a primary or indexed source are marked **unverified — do not use**.

**How sources were verified**
- *PubMed-indexed*: title, authors, journal, year, and abstract retrieved from PubMed metadata (DOI given).
- *Consensus-indexed*: record retrieved via Consensus (Semantic Scholar/OpenAlex/PubMed index); link given is the Consensus record. Where a DOI was not returned, none is listed — do not invent one.
- *Agency page*: figure read directly from the agency web page or PDF (URL given).
- *Secondary only*: figure seen only in news/summary coverage, primary document not opened. Use with care or avoid.

Landmark pre-2018 studies are labeled **[landmark, pre-2018]**.

**Pitch framing note:** Safest headline: "Teach-back is a recommended, low-cost strategy with meta-analytic evidence for better adherence and self-efficacy, and a 45% lower 30-day readmission odds in pooled discharge studies." Aftercare Loop's teach-back is a *digital adaptation*; no study above tested an AI-delivered version — do not imply they did.

**Positioning sentence:** Incumbents either help the *clinician* document the visit (Abridge, Epic drafting) or help the *consumer* decide whether to seek care (Ada, K Health). ## Items that could not be verified — do not use

- **"As many as 40% of women do not attend a postpartum visit" attributed to ACOG CO 736.** Primary text paywalled; abstract does not contain it; only blogs repeat it. Substitute Rodin 2019 (<60% of Medicaid enrollees attend) or Wilcox 2016 (one-third non-attendance).
- **National PRAMS postpartum-checkup rate (≈90%) and national Medicaid HEDIS PPC rate.** Only state-level reports and a summary of a PRAMS analysis were found; no national primary figure retrieved. Note that self-reported PRAMS rates (~86–91% in state reports) are much higher than Medicaid claims-based rates — do not mix them.
- **"About 700 pregnancy-related deaths per year."** Not on the CDC pages retrieved; use the MMRC counts (525 deaths in 38 states, 2020) or the percentages instead.
- **Vernon 2007 $106–238B** — widely repeated and consistent across summaries, but the primary GWU PDF was not opened. Label as "estimated (2007)" if used.
- **HRSA press-release figures (16,000+ sites; 1 in 5 rural residents)** — the page returned HTTP 403; figures came from search-indexed text of the release. The UDS portal numbers (32,387,774 patients, poverty/uninsured percentages) were read directly and are safe.
- **Epic MyChart 2025 AI roadmap details (result summaries, 180 customers using drafting assistant)** — trade-press only; Epic's own post confirms only that "built-in AI assistants will help answer questions."
- **Any claim that teach-back delivered by an AI companion improves outcomes.** No such study was found; all teach-back evidence is clinician-delivered. Say "adapts a clinician technique with meta-analytic support," never "clinically validated."
- **Any claim that women have lower health literacy than men.** NAAL shows the reverse (12% vs 16% Below Basic).
